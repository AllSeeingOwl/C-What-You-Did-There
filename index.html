Side C(What You Did There)

Design mockups for a site where mixtapes are made of everything: instructions for hunting down moments from radio, TV, film, video and podcasts, then playing them back. Most screens are clickable.

  1. 01Welcome and sign-upSix steps: the idea, account, region, vibes, reading style, a first tape.
  2. 02Home and browseWeekly prompt, explore by region, filters for length, vibe and availability.
  3. 03Tape landing pageJ-card, liner notes, vibe arc, what you'll need, running order.
  4. 04Tape timelineThe editing-suite view for collecting slots, with clues and fallbacks.
  5. 05Timeline on a phoneA vertical timeline with a bottom sheet for each slot.
  6. 06Play-along and revealStep through the tape with timers, Interference, then the creator's notes.
  7. 07Write a tapeThe submission form: vibes, tier, slots, fallbacks, regions.
