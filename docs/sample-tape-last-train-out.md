# Last Train Out

A Side C sample sync tape · Last updated 22 November 2026

## For testers: how this works

This is a sync tape: instructions for a home-made music video. You choose a song that fits the brief, hunt down a set of short clips, then play the song with sound while you cut the clips in over it, muted. Nothing is attached and nothing gets downloaded. You find everything yourself from official sources.

1. Read **The tape at a glance** and **Choosing your song**, then pick your song.
2. Listen to it once and fill in **Map your song**: the time each section starts.
3. Hunt down each scene at your own pace, using **The running order**. Note what you found and your start time in the last column.
4. Once everything is collected, follow **Playing it back** in one go.
5. Then read **The reveal** and answer the questions at the end.

This tape never quotes any lyrics, and you shouldn't write them down either. Where a scene depends on the words, it asks you to listen for them.

## Choosing your song

Choose any song that fits all of these:

- It's about leaving, moving on or going away, in any genre and from any year.
- It runs 2:30 to 4:30.
- It has verses and at least two choruses. A bridge (a different-sounding part near the end) is a bonus, not a must.
- You can play it from an official source: the artist's own channel, a streaming service, radio catch-up or a copy you own.

Write down the song and the exact version you're using (for example "album version, 3:34"), so you play the same one every time.

## The running order

Each scene plays, muted, from its section's start time until the next one. Pick clips that run a little longer than the section, so you never run out. If a clip does run out, pause on its last frame.

### Fallbacks

If a scene is hard to find, any of these counts as collected.

- **Scene 2:** (A) any advert where someone sits on a suitcase to close it; (B) pack a bag yourself, badly, and do it live during the scene.
- **Scene 3:** (A) if your verse has nothing concrete in it, use a clip of the song's title, taken literally; (B) any road sign pointing somewhere far away.
- **Scene 4:** (A) if the key word can't be acted out (like "maybe"), use a clip of someone shrugging; (B) hold up a hand-written card with a drawing of the word.
- **Scene 5:** (A) any time-lapse of clouds moving across the sky; (B) any shot of an aeroplane taking off.
- **Scene 8:** (A) any closing credits rolling at the end of a children's programme; (B) any announcer saying goodnight before a channel closes down.
- **Scene 9:** (A) any boat leaving a harbour; (B) the back of any bus driving away down a street.

## Playing it back

Play everything in one go, without stopping the song. It's easiest with two devices.

1. Set up first:
    1. Put the song on one device (a phone, speaker or radio) and turn the volume up.
    2. Open every clip on another screen (a laptop or TV), each muted and cued to your start time, in its own tab.
    3. Keep **Map your song** where you can see it, along with a clock or timer that shows seconds.
2. Dim the lights.
3. Start the song and the timer at the same moment.
4. At each section's start time, switch to the next scene's tab and press play. Scenes 7 and 10 replay earlier tabs, so restart them from your start time.
5. At scene 6, turn off the light and stay on the scene 5 clip until chorus 2.
6. Hold the last picture until the song ends, then sit for a moment before reading on.

Being a second or two late is fine. A slightly wonky cut is part of the charm.
