# Message for mixtape testers

Last updated 24 November 2026

A ready-to-send message for friends testing the sample mixtape, *Rain on a Tuesday*. Change the bits in [SQUARE BRACKETS] and send it by text, email or chat.

---

Hi [NAME],

I'm working on an idea called **Side C** and I'd love you to try one of its **mixtapes**. A Side C mixtape is made of everything: short moments from radio, TV, film, videos and podcasts, plus a few small things to do in the room. The catch is that you hunt each piece down yourself, then play the whole mixtape back in one sitting.

Here's the test mixtape, **Rain on a Tuesday**: https://allseeingowl.github.io/C-What-You-Did-There/#/tape/sample-rain-on-a-tuesday

1. **Collect the 9 pieces at your own pace,** over a few days or a week. The site tells you what to find and where to look, and records what you found. If something's hard to get, use a fallback; it still counts.
2. **Play it back in one go** once you've got everything. It's about 8 minutes. Lights low is best.
3. **Don't peek at the creator's notes** until the end. That's the reveal.
4. **Answer the 8 short questions** that appear after the reveal, then press "Copy my answers" and send them to me. If you give up partway, there's a link on its page to answer them anyway, which is just as useful.

Good to know:

- Nothing to download or install, and no account. It works on a phone or a computer.
- Your progress is saved in that browser, so carry on in the same browser on the same device.
- Only use official sources (catch-up apps, official channels, services you already have). No dodgy copies, please.

Thanks! [YOUR NAME]

---

## Notes for you

- Ask testers to keep to one device and browser, as nothing syncs between them yet.
- The answers arrive as plain text in whatever you send them through, so make a note of who said what.
- When feedback starts to repeat itself, it's time to change the design.
- Side C also has music videos. Their tester message is in `tester-message-sync-tape.md`. Someone who has tried this mixtape is a good person to test one next.
