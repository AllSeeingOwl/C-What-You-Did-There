# Message for music video testers

Last updated 24 November 2026

A ready-to-send message for friends testing the sample music video, *Last Train Out*. Change the bits in [SQUARE BRACKETS] and send it by text, email or chat. It works best with someone who has already tried *Rain on a Tuesday*, but it doesn't have to be.

---

Hi [NAME],

I'm trying out **music videos** on **Side C** and I'd love you to test one. It's a home-made music video: you pick a song, hunt down some short clips, then play the song out loud while you cut the clips in over the top, muted, so the pictures "explain" the song (often far too literally).

Here's the test music video, **Last Train Out**: https://allseeingowl.github.io/C-What-You-Did-There/#/tape/sample-last-train-out

The site walks you through it and saves your progress in that browser, so carry on in the same browser on the same device.

1. **Pick your song.** Any song about leaving or going away, 2:30 to 4:30 long, with at least two choruses. The site explains the rest.
2. **Map your song.** Listen once and note when each part starts (verse, chorus and so on) on the site. It takes about five minutes, and it's what times your cuts.
3. **Collect the 9 clips at your own pace,** over a few days or a week. Three of them depend on the words of your song, which is the fun part. If something's hard to find, use a fallback; it still counts.
4. **Play it back in one go.** The song goes on one device (phone or speaker), the clips on another (laptop or TV), with the clips muted. The site's song clock tells you when to cut. Lights low is best.
5. **Don't peek at the creator's notes** until the end. That's the reveal.
6. **Answer the 10 short questions** that appear after the reveal, then press "Copy my answers" and send them to me. If you give up partway, there's a link on its page to answer them anyway, which is just as useful.

Good to know:

- Nothing to download or install, and no account. It works on a phone or a computer.
- Only use official sources (streaming services, catch-up apps, official channels, things you already own). No dodgy copies, please.
- Please don't write out any of the song's lyrics in your answers. Describe them in your own words instead.
- Keep your clips varied: never the same clip twice, and no more than two from the same film or show. The site explains why.
- Being a second or two late with a cut is fine. Wonky is part of the charm.

Thanks! [YOUR NAME]

---

## Notes for you

- If someone would rather not use the website, send them the written version instead: https://github.com/AllSeeingOwl/C-What-You-Did-There/blob/main/docs/sample-tape-last-train-out.md
- Ask which song each tester picked. Comparing videos made from different songs is the main thing this test is for.
- Watch especially for answers to "too fiddly?" and "two devices?". They show whether the song clock is enough, or whether music videos need more help.
