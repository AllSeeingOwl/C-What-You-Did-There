# Rain on a Tuesday

A Side C sample tape · Last updated 7 November 2026

## For testers: how this works

This is a real, playable Side C tape: collect its 12 pieces over a week or so, play it back in one sitting, then tell us how it went. Nothing is attached and nothing gets downloaded. The tape is only instructions, and you find each piece yourself from official sources.

1. Read **The tape at a glance** and **The running order**. Don't read **The reveal** yet.
2. Hunt down each slot at your own pace. When you find one, note what it was and your start and end times in the last column of the running order.
3. Stuck, or something isn't available where you live? Use a fallback. It still counts.
4. Once all 12 are collected, follow **Playing it back** in one sitting, ideally with the lights low.
5. Then read **The reveal** and answer the questions at the end.

Most slots in this sample say "any" (any weather bulletin, any long rain video), so you choose the exact clip. That keeps it playable wherever you are. Aim for each slot's length, but within a few seconds either way is fine; the whole tape can run anywhere from about 7:15 to 8:45.

### Fallbacks

If a slot is hard to find or not available where you are, any of these counts as collected.

- **Slot 2:** (A) any slow, calm shipping or travel bulletin; (B) a sleep or relaxation track with a spoken weather report.
- **Slot 7:** (A) any live TV moment where a presenter corpses on air; (B) a newsreader trying to keep a straight face over a silly story.
- **Slot 8:** (A) any radio drama or podcast scene about lights in the sky; (B) a documentary interview with someone describing a sighting; (C) read a short eyewitness-style account aloud yourself.
- **Slot 9:** (A) sound: any calm shipping-style forecast over the same video; (B) picture: any upbeat holiday advert, muted.

## Playing it back

Play all 12 slots in one sitting, in order, with as little gap between them as you can manage.

1. Set up first: open every source in its own tab or app, cued to your start time, with this page and a timer beside you.
2. Dim the lights and sit somewhere you can see a screen and hear clearly.
3. For each clip slot, play from your start time and stop at your end time; use the slot's length as a guide.
4. For the in-between slots (1, 5 and 11), do what they say, and time them.
5. For slot 9, Interference:
    1. Start the music video with the sound off.
    2. Watch for the sync cue: the singer's first smile at the camera.
    3. Start the weather warning at that moment and let both run for 40 seconds.
6. After slot 12, sit for a moment before reading on.

Small gaps while you switch sources are fine. The tape is about the flow, not split-second timing.
